Common Questions

AI Agents & Automation FAQ

How long does it take to build an automation or AI agent? ▾
A single workflow automation typically takes 1–2 weeks. A custom AI agent or a connected multi-workflow system generally takes 3–5 weeks depending on complexity, integrations, and feedback turnaround.
Will we own the automations and workflows? ▾
Yes — upon final payment you get full ownership of every workflow, agent configuration, and integration we build. Your automations live in your own accounts, with no lock-in.
Can you integrate with our existing CRM and tools? ▾
Absolutely. We work with whatever stack you already have — HubSpot, Salesforce, Airtable, Slack, Google Workspace, and most tools with an API — and connect them into a single automated system.
What happens if our processes change later? ▾
Automations are built to be adjusted. As your processes evolve we can update logic, add new integrations, or extend your AI agents — most changes are quick since the foundation is already in place.
