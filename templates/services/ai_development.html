Common Questions

AI development FAQ

Can you build the entire product from scratch? ▾
Yes. We can handle the complete product journey — from architecture and UI/UX to frontend, backend, AI integration, testing, deployment, and ongoing improvements.
Can you add AI to an existing application? ▾
Absolutely. We can integrate AI capabilities into an existing product, codebase, backend, or workflow without requiring you to rebuild everything from scratch.
Can you integrate with our existing product or data? ▾
Absolutely. We regularly build on top of existing codebases and connect to your existing databases, APIs, and internal tools rather than starting from scratch.
Which AI models do you work with? ▾
We work across OpenAI, Anthropic's Claude, and Google's Gemini, and we'll recommend the model (or combination of models) that best fits your accuracy, cost, and latency needs.
