Common Questions

AI Development FAQ

How long does it take to build a custom AI application? ▾
A focused LLM feature or RAG system typically takes 2–4 weeks. A full AI SaaS product generally takes 6–12 weeks depending on scope, integrations, and feedback turnaround.
Will we own the AI application and its IP? ▾
Yes — upon final payment you own the full codebase, architecture, and any custom models or prompts we build. There's no lock-in to us as a vendor.
Can you integrate with our existing product or data? ▾
Absolutely. We regularly build on top of existing codebases and connect to your existing databases, APIs, and internal tools rather than starting from scratch.
Which AI models do you work with? ▾
We work across OpenAI, Anthropic's Claude, and Google's Gemini, and we'll recommend the model (or combination of models) that best fits your accuracy, cost, and latency needs.
