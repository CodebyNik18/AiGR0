Common Questions

AI Agents & Agentic Systems FAQ

What's the difference between an AI agent and simple automation? ▾
Traditional automation follows a fixed, pre-defined path. An agentic AI system reasons about the task, decides what to do next, and can adapt its approach — closer to how a person would handle an open-ended job.
Can your agents actually take actions, or just generate text? ▾
They take real actions. We connect agents to your APIs, databases, and tools so they can send emails, update records, query data, or trigger workflows — not just describe what should happen.
How do you keep autonomous agents safe and controlled? ▾
Every agent is built with clear boundaries — which tools it can call, what actions require human approval, and logging on every decision — so autonomy never means unpredictability.
Will we own the agents you build? ▾
Yes — upon final payment you own the full agent configuration, prompts, and integrations. There's no lock-in to us as a vendor.
