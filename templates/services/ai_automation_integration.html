Common Questions

AI Automation & Integrations FAQ

How long does it take to build an automation? ▾
A single workflow automation typically takes 1–2 weeks. A fully connected multi-tool integration system generally takes 3–5 weeks depending on complexity.
Will we own the automations and integrations you build? ▾
Yes — upon final payment you own every workflow, integration, and configuration. Everything lives in your own accounts, with no lock-in.
Can you connect to our existing CRM and tools? ▾
Absolutely. We work with whatever stack you already have — most CRMs, databases, and any tool with an API or webhook support — and connect them into one automated system.
What happens if our processes change later? ▾
Automations are built to be adjusted. As your processes evolve we can update logic, add new integrations, or extend existing workflows — most changes are quick since the foundation is already in place.
