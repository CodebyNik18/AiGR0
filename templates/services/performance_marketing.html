Common Questions

Performance Marketing FAQ

How much should I budget for performance marketing? ▾
It depends on your industry, competition, and goals. We work with budgets as low as $1,000/month across channels for local businesses and scale up for competitive national campaigns. We'll recommend a realistic budget in your free consultation.
How quickly will I see results? ▾
Paid campaigns can drive traffic and conversions from day one. Most accounts see meaningful performance improvements within the first 2–4 weeks as we gather data and optimize.
Do you manage Google and Meta together or separately? ▾
Together, as one connected strategy. We look at performance across channels holistically and shift budget to whichever is driving the strongest return, rather than managing each in isolation.
Will I have access to my own ad accounts? ▾
Absolutely. You own your Google Ads and Meta Ads accounts 100%. We operate as a manager under your accounts, giving you full transparency at any time.
