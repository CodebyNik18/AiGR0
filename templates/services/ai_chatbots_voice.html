Common Questions

AI Chatbots & Voice AI FAQ

How long does it take to build a chatbot or voice agent? ▾
A standard support chatbot or WhatsApp AI typically takes 1–2 weeks. A custom RAG chatbot or Retell voice agent generally takes 3–4 weeks depending on the complexity of your knowledge base and call flows.
Will it sound natural, not robotic? ▾
Yes. We tune tone, pacing, and phrasing for both chat and voice agents so conversations feel natural and on-brand, not like a generic scripted bot.
Can it integrate with WhatsApp or our existing phone system? ▾
Yes — we connect directly to WhatsApp Business API and to your existing phone lines through Retell AI and Twilio, so there's no need to change your current numbers or systems.
What happens when the bot doesn't know the answer? ▾
Every agent is built with clear escalation logic — when a question falls outside its knowledge or confidence, it hands the conversation off to a human teammate instead of guessing.
